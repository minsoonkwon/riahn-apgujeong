RIAHN Hair BLACK Apgujeong Rodeo

RIAHN Hair BLACK Apgujeong Rodeo is a hair salon at 3F, 25 Seolleung-ro 157-gil, Sinsa-dong, Gangnam-gu, Seoul, a 5-minute walk from Apgujeong Rodeo Station Exit 5.

Opened in 2024 with 6 styling chairs, our designers consult with you one-on-one before cuts, perms, color, treatments and hair extensions (U-tip and no-tip). We also offer scalp detox and head spa programs.

Free parking and free valet parking are available. We are open Tuesday to Sunday 10:00–20:00 and closed every Monday.

Why RIAHN Hair BLACK Apgujeong Rodeo

  • Free parking and free valet parking.
  • U-tip and no-tip hair extensions, with retouch and removal services.
  • A designer specializing in men's hair offers dandy cuts and custom down perms.
  • Scalp and hair care programs such as scalp detox and head spa.
  • 10% off with Naver Pay or a review, plus membership discounts by amount.
