Hair Color

Prices for root and full color (Milbon, Shiseido), bleach and design color.

Home/Hair Color

From root color
to ash brown and matte brown

At RIAHN Hair BLACK Apgujeong Rodeo, root color (up to 3 cm) is ₩60,000 Basic and ₩80,000 Premium (Shiseido). Full color is ₩120,000 Basic (Milbon) and ₩150,000 Premium (Shiseido).

Full bleach is ₩160,000 and root bleach ₩100,000. Design color such as ombré, sombré, highlights and two-tone starts from ₩180,000, confirmed after consultation. Designer Gahyeon (Autumn) specializes in ash brown and Designer Kwon Hyuk in matte brown, and Designer Jaehyeong has her own price list starting at ₩80,000 for root color. Your designer consults with you one-on-one first, then works to suit your head shape and hair texture.

Recommended for

  • Anyone who just wants to touch up grown-out roots
  • Anyone who wants ash brown or matte brown color
  • Anyone who wants full color with Shiseido products
  • Anyone preparing for a bright shade or design color with bleach

FAQ

Hair Color FAQ

How much is full color?
Full color is ₩120,000 Basic (Milbon) and ₩150,000 Premium (Shiseido), or ₩108,000 and ₩135,000 with Naver Pay or a review.
How much is root color?
Root color (up to 3 cm) is ₩60,000 Basic and ₩80,000 Premium (Shiseido). Root bleach is ₩100,000.
How much are bleach and design color?
Full bleach is ₩160,000 (₩144,000 with Naver Pay or a review), and design color starts from ₩180,000, confirmed after consultation.
