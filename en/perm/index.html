Perms

Prices for cozy and air perms, mood perm (straightening / volume straightening), glow perm, and root, bangs and down perms.

Home/Perms

From cozy and air perms
to volume straightening and down perms

At RIAHN Hair BLACK Apgujeong Rodeo, the cozy perm (regular perm) Basic is ₩100,000 and Design Perm ₩130,000. The air perm (setting perm) is ₩170,000 Basic, ₩210,000 Special and ₩240,000 Premium.

The mood perm (straightening / volume straightening) is ₩220,000, the glow perm (straightening + setting perm) ₩270,000 and the sleek root perm (root straightening) ₩130,000. Partial perms include the root tension perm, fringe perm (bangs perm) and line fix perm (down perm). Designer Jaehyeong has her own price list, starting at ₩132,000 for a cozy perm. Your designer consults with you one-on-one first, then works to suit your head shape and hair texture. To change your color too, see Hair Color.

Recommended for

  • Anyone who wants smooth hair with volume from volume straightening
  • Anyone who wants natural waves from an air perm or Az perm
  • Men who want to tame puffy sides or back with a down perm
  • Anyone who wants a perm only at the roots or on the bangs

FAQ

Perm FAQ

How much is volume straightening?
The mood perm (straightening / volume straightening) is ₩220,000, or ₩198,000 with Naver Pay or a review. The glow perm (straightening + setting perm) is ₩270,000.
How much is a men's down perm?
The line fix perm (down perm) is ₩40,000 for the sides, ₩50,000 for the sides and back, and ₩70,000 for the full head. A men's cut + line fix perm (side down perm) is ₩50,000.
Is there a surcharge for long hair?
Yes. By length, S has no surcharge, M is +₩20,000, L is +₩40,000 and LL is +₩60,000. Please confirm your length at your consultation.
