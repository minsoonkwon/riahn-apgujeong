FAQ

FAQ

Answers to common questions about booking, parking, hours, last booking times, prices, discounts and hair extensions.

Home/FAQ
How do I book?
You can book your preferred date and time on Naver Booking. You can also book or ask questions by phone (+82-2-6941-1554).
Is parking available?
Yes. Free parking and free valet parking are available.
What are your hours and days off?
We are open Tuesday to Sunday 10:00–20:00 and closed every Monday.
Where is the salon?
3F, 25 Seolleung-ro 157-gil, Gangnam-gu, Seoul (Sinsa-dong). It is a 5-minute walk from Apgujeong Rodeo Station Exit 5.
What time is the last booking?
Cuts until 19:30; cut + down perm, root color and treatments until 19:00; full color and men's perms until 18:30; perms until 18:00. If you need another time, please call the salon (+82-2-6941-1554).
What are your prices?
Men's design cut ₩25,000, women's design cut ₩30,000, cozy perm (regular perm) ₩100,000, mood perm (volume straightening) ₩220,000, root color ₩60,000, full color ₩120,000 and scalp detox ₩50,000. Designer Jaehyeong has her own price list. The larger amount in the price list is the regular price. The final price can vary with the service, hair volume and length, and is confirmed at your consultation.
Do you offer discounts?
Yes. First-time guests get 30% off perms, color and treatments (excluding cuts, blow-dry and partial services; with Naver Pay and a photo review) and a down perm + men's cut for ₩35,000. After that, perms, color and treatments are 10% off with Naver Pay or a review. Discounts cannot be combined, and Designer Jaehyeong's menu is not eligible for first-visit discounts. We also offer membership discounts by amount; please ask the salon for details.
Is there a surcharge for long hair?
Yes. By length, S has no surcharge, M is +₩20,000, L is +₩40,000 and LL is +₩60,000. Please confirm your length at your consultation.
Do you do hair extensions?
Yes. We offer U-tip and no-tip extensions, plus retouches (₩150,000 per session, up to 2 times) and removal (₩50,000 for extensions done at our salon, ₩80,000 for those done elsewhere).
Can I book a specific designer?
Please choose your preferred designer when you book. For Az perms and ash brown we recommend Designer Gahyeon (Autumn); for dandy cuts and custom down perms, Designer Kwon Hyuk; for updos and Rapunzel repair care, Designer Jaehyeong.

Didn't find your answer? Call us during opening hours (Tue–Sun 10:00–20:00).
