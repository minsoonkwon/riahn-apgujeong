Treatments & Head Spa

Prices for scalp detox and fresh head spa, plus hair vitamin, glossy care and mood glow care.

Home/Treatments & Head Spa

From scalp detox and head spa
to glossy care

At RIAHN Hair BLACK Apgujeong Rodeo, the scalp detox (₩50,000) is a quick scalp care session of up to 30 minutes using Ayunche scalp scaling products, and the fresh head spa (₩100,000) combines scalp scaling, mist, a scalp massager, hand acupressure, a scalp pack and a Dohi spa.

Hair care options are hair vitamin (treatment shampoo + 1 moisture step) ₩50,000, glossy care (6 steps) ₩150,000 and mood glow care (Shiseido full care) ₩180,000. Designer Jaehyeong offers a head spa with lymph and shoulder massage (₩120,000) and Rapunzel repair care (₩250,000), and Partner Soyeon helps with head spa and shampoo. To combine with a perm or color, see Perms.

Recommended for

  • Anyone who wants their scalp to feel refreshed
  • Anyone who wants a relaxing head spa
  • Anyone who wants to smooth their hair after a perm or color
