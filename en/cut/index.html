From dandy and layered cuts
to U-tip and no-tip extensions

At RIAHN Hair BLACK Apgujeong Rodeo, men's design cuts and student cuts are ₩25,000 and women's design cuts are ₩30,000. Cuts with scalp scaling are ₩35,000 for men and ₩40,000 for women. A men's cut + line fix perm (side down perm) is ₩50,000, and Designer Kwon Hyuk offers a full down perm + men's cut (₩55,000) and a version with a volume perm (₩70,000). Your designer consults with you one-on-one first, then works to suit your head shape and hair texture.

We offer U-tip extensions (16, 18 and 20-inch full pieces, ₩340,000–₩380,000) and no-tip extensions (18 and 20-inch full pieces, ₩460,000–₩480,000), plus half pieces, volume add-ons, retouches (₩150,000 per session, up to 2 times) and removal. For more volume, see Perms.

Recommended for

  • Anyone who wants a dandy or layered cut that suits their head shape
  • Men who want a cut and down perm in one visit
  • Anyone who wants to add length and volume with U-tip or no-tip extensions
  • Anyone who wants blow-dry or iron styling before a special occasion

Cuts & Extensions FAQ

How much is a men's cut?
A men's design cut is ₩25,000 and a men's cut with scalp scaling is ₩35,000. A men's cut + line fix perm (side down perm) is ₩50,000, and on your first visit you can get a down perm + men's cut for ₩35,000 (with Naver Pay and a review).
How much is a women's cut?
A women's design cut is ₩30,000 and a women's cut with scalp scaling is ₩40,000. Student cuts are ₩25,000 and a bangs trim is ₩10,000.
How much are hair extensions?
U-tip full pieces are ₩340,000 (16 in), ₩360,000 (18 in) and ₩380,000 (20 in). No-tip full pieces are ₩460,000 (18 in) and ₩480,000 (20 in). A retouch is ₩150,000 per session (up to 2 times).
